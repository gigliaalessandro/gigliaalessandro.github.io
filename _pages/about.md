I am a Doctoral Research Fellow in Philosophy at the University of Oslo, supervised by Professor Øystein Linnebo. My position is funded by the ERC project [C-FORS: Construction in the Formal Sciences](https://cfors.org/). From April to June 2026 I was a visiting Recognised Student at the University of Oxford, working with James Studd.

Before coming to Oslo, I completed an MA in Philosophy at the Università della Svizzera italiana in Lugano and a BA in Philosophy at the Università Cattolica del Sacro Cuore in Milan.
