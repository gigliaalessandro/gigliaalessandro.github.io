I am a Doctoral Research Fellow in Philosophy at the University of Oslo, supervised by Professor Øystein Linnebo. My position is funded by the ERC project [C-FORS: Construction in the Formal Sciences](https://cfors.org/). From April to June 2026 I was a visiting Recognised Student at the University of Oxford, working with James Studd.

I work in the philosophy of mathematics and the philosophy of logic. My research focuses on potentialism in set theory: the view that the hierarchy of sets is never complete, since any collection of sets can always be extended further. My dissertation asks how potentialist set theories should be interpreted, and how they compare with their actualist counterparts, on which all sets exist at once. Questions I work on include whether potentialist and actualist set theories are equivalent, and what role the domain principle and variable quantities play in arguments about potential infinity. See [Research](/research/) for my current papers.

Before coming to Oslo, I completed an MA in Philosophy at the Università della Svizzera italiana in Lugano and a BA in Philosophy at the Università Cattolica del Sacro Cuore in Milan.
