I am a Doctoral Research Fellow in Philosophy at the University of Oslo, supervised by Professor Øystein Linnebo. My project is funded by the ERC project [C-FORS: Construction in the Formal Sciences](https://cfors.org/). 

My research is in philosophy of mathematics and philosophy of logic, with a focus on interpretability, modal logic, and set theory. 

Before coming to Oslo, I completed an MA in Philosophy at the Università della Svizzera italiana in Lugano and a BA in Philosophy at the Università Cattolica del Sacro Cuore in Milan.

Outside philosophy, I practise Muay Thai, go bouldering and play chess, all as an enthusiastic amateur.
